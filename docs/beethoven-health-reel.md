# The Beethoven's Health reel

Where the project stands at the end of the Pi session of 19 October 2026, so
the laptop session (which has Claude in Chrome and can open Instagram) can pick
it up without redoing the research. Method and the reference reels are in
[`format-research.md`](format-research.md).

## The brief

Make a reel from the Beethoven's Health material in the vault — 127 quotations
chosen for colour from Thayer–Forbes, 69 in Beethoven's own words, each tied to
the page it comes from and to the work in hand at the time — for an audience
that already knows Beethoven was deaf and does not mind an irreverent take.
Matthew's own account (@matthewlorenzon, 8K followers on Instagram; a fresh
TikTok). Face and voice available; piano not. Scope (one reel or a series)
left open for the research to settle.

The material, in the vault at `Symphony of the day/Beethoven's Health/`:

## What the research settled

Four web-research threads ran in parallel (formats, classical-music
benchmarks, platform mechanics, comic register), then twelve reference reels
were read frame-by-frame and transcribed. Full reports with dated sources are in
the vault note; the findings that bear on the build:

**On the platform (as of Oct 2026).** Instagram's originality rules were
extended to photos and carousels on 30 Apr 2026: archival images re-uploaded
with a credit are not original; your own narration, on-image text and editing
are. Reel engagement and median views peak at 45–60 s in the 2026 brand data,
with little difference 30 s–3 min and a cliff after 3 min. Carousels get the
highest engagement rate and ~9× the saves of a single image, but on TikTok
photo-mode gets a fifth of video's reach: carousel = saves, reel = reach.
Trial Reels (non-followers first, invisible to followers, auto-share if it
performs) need ~200 followers on a professional account; Matthew qualifies.
Trending audio appears in no published ranking signal; the one controlled test
found no reach difference. For Beethoven under a reel, a CC0 recording
uploaded as original audio is the clean route (Musopen's cycle — verify the
licence on the download page).

**On register.** "Lore" is Sprout's 2026 word of the year — the structure is
rewarded, the word is saturated. "Unhinged" is fatigued (Duolingo retreated,
Apr 2026). Hopecore is in a sincere second wave, so parodying it reads as 2024.
Sincere-then-undercut works when the undercut is a quotation, not your joke.
Slang in overlays is out; AI voice or imagery actively hurts a history piece
(~34% of history in TikTok's new-user feed is AI slop, Kapwing May 2026).
"Classical over chaos" and ironic Symphony 5 / Für Elise / Ode to Joy are
documented clichés; period-correct music is both on-joke and fresh.

**On the field.** Beethoven's health is unclaimed: no viral short was found on
the 2023/2024 hair studies, and existing Beethoven memes cover the scowl, the
Fifth, Moonlight, Für Elise and "deaf hero" only. Primary-source medical
grossness carries its own audience (Suzie Edge 380K TikTok; Lindsey Fitzharris
~292K IG with one morbid fact a day). Nobody at scale does composer *history*
with humour, and nobody at 100K+ reads historical letters for laughs. Horrible
Histories has announced a Beethoven episode with the BBC Concert Orchestra
(Oct 2026), so the gleeful register will be occupied in 2027. **The death
bicentenary is 26 March 2027**; the 1827 picks end on exactly that date.

**On the two sketches.** (2) is dropped in its styling but its hook survives
as a correction rather than a question. (1) is *not* dropped: The Gaze does
exactly that — paintings, dry voiceover, typographic captions on a house colour
— at 5.3M with no face. What it adds to the sketch is a voice that treats the
image as evidence and an editorial structure (a refrain, details in sequence, a
closing question). The irony is the dry voice against the grandeur, not the
music against the picture.

## A count that killed a hook

"He wrote more about his gut than his ears" was going to be the line. It is
false by the vault's own tagging. Beethoven's own-words quotations per
condition in *by Condition*: general illness 125, **deafness 69**, despair 63,
convalescence 41, **abdominal 25**, final illness 23, eyes 16. What the
material does support is his own weighing of the two — 1801: "my belly
improved, but my deafness remained and even became worse" (Thayer–Forbes
p. 284); 1787: melancholy "almost as great an evil as my malady itself"
(p. 89) — and the doctors, the richest treatments category: physician 126
entries, rest/country air 70, bath/spa 69, diet 49, alcohol 44, medicine 37.

So the honest Neely-style correction is about the doctors: *"You know
Beethoven went deaf. You don't know what his doctors did about it."* Then the
page.

## The shape the reference reels point to

Matthew's own 3.5M reel already has the pattern the twelve converged on: a
real person first, a real object doing the work, low polish, a borrowed
structure for comic shape (the stages of grief), a payoff, and a reason to
send. For the Beethoven material:

- **First line is the thesis, in Matthew's words**, not a question. Payoff by
  10 s: the quote as proof.
- **The object is the Thayer–Forbes volume** on the lap, open at the page,
  finger on the line — the breadboard. A yellow underline on the line the way
  The Gaze circles a detail.
- **The series card**, persistent at the top like Porter's: *Beethoven's
  Health, 1801.* The vault is already organised by year, so the series is the
  folder; 32 year pages.
- **The request loop:** "Which year next?" costs nothing and maps to work
  already done.
- **The quotes are read, not displayed** (Letters Live). Matthew reading them
  to camera, or Matthew's face reacting to them, does what the laughing crowd
  does.
- **Period images from the Slideshow note** — Gillray's and Cruikshank's
  colics, the Stainhauser portrait, the Danube, Heiligenstadt — with Matthew's
  voiceover and two-tier captions on a house colour, for the stretches between
  the face.
- **The music is period-correct and under**, never blaring: the work in the
  *composing at the time* column is the evidence that he wrote this while
  living like that.
- **Length:** the data's 45–60 s is where the pilot should land; the reference
  reels show the audience will stay longer if every layer pays.

## The 1801 pilot: the spine

Built on a refrain — *"Did it work?"* — the way *Calvary* is built on "But
where is Christ?". Every line is Beethoven's or Thayer's, from the Picks and
Treatments tables; page numbers are Thayer–Forbes printed pages.

1. Frank: tonics for the body, almond oil for the ears. *"But prosit, nothing
   happened."* (p. 283)
2. *"Then came a medical ass who advised me to take cold baths for my
   health."* Did it work? (p. 284)
3. *"A more sensible one advised the usual lukewarm Danube bath… That worked
   wonders, my belly improved, but my deafness remained and even became
   worse."* (p. 284)
4. Vering: pills for the stomach, *"a kind of herb for my ear"*; then
   *"vesicatories placed on both my arms… I am robbed of the free use of my
   arms for a few days."* (pp. 284, 286)
5. The turn: what he was writing with those arms — the Second Symphony, the
   Op. 28 sonata — and *"Courage! In spite of all bodily weaknesses my spirit
   shall rule."* (p. 182)

**Check before shooting:** the "medical ass" letter is dated 29 June 1800 by
one web source and November 1801 by another; the Picks table has 29 June 1801
against p. 284 (PDF page 310). Confirm against the page. Also confirm the Op. 28
dating against Grove the way the symphony records are confirmed.

Alternatives held in reserve: the 1812 Teplitz spa run ("splash around in the
water again", "the tyrant is chained to his bed like a slave") with the Eighth
as payoff; the 1827 deathbed sequence timed to the bicentenary, a sincerer
piece.

## Open decisions

1. **Hook register:** flat and specific vs the deafness correction above.
   Both are honest; the correction spends two seconds on what the audience
   knows, the flat line does not.
2. **Face reading vs voiceover over images** for the quotes. Test both as
   Trial Reels; the account qualifies.
3. **Series or one reel.** Everything in the material and in the reference
   set says series — one year, one reel, 32 of them — with the 1801 pilot
   first. Not yet decided.
4. **TikTok and Shorts as second homes.** YouTube cut Shorts reach for
   re-uploaders on 1 Oct 2026; original commentary counts as original. Post a
   clean, unwatermarked master.
