## Caption

🩰I LOVE paintings with hidden surprises. “The Swing” was painted around 1767 by Jean-Honoré Fragonard, one of the great French Rococo painters. 💃🏼 The patron that commissioned this work asked the artist: “I want you to paint my mistress on a swing, pushed by a bishop and to show me in a position where I can see her legs and more.” And Fragonard nailed it, apart from the bishop, which he replaced by a regular old man. ❄️ Throughout history “The Swing” has been referenced and parodied in poems, album artwork, contemporary art and even in Disney’s Frozen.  #arthistory #fragonard #artmemes #artexplained #arttok
