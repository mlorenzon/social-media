## Caption

The first symphony by a female composer (that we have the score for) was by Marianna Martines in Vienna in 1770. A young Haydn lived in her attic and taught her musical rudiments while Mozart attended her family's parties. I wonder if she and Nannerl got along. The first female composers in a genre appear pretty much right alongside their male counterparts. This is because, surprise, surprise, they're there all along. This Sinfonia/Overture/Symphony is a delight and thank you to La Floridiana under Nicoleta Paraschivescu (Harpsichord and Conductor) for the recording used here.  #Symphony #MusicAppreciation #ClassicalMusic #WomenInMusic
